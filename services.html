Where every visit starts

Consultation

Every new patient starts with a thorough consultation. Jason reviews your pain history, posture, and daily movement patterns to understand what’s really driving your discomfort — not just where it hurts.

From there, he maps out a treatment plan tailored to your body, your goals, and your lifestyle — drawing on whichever of the four treatments below your body actually needs.

  • Posture and movement assessment
  • Pain history and lifestyle review
  • Personalised treatment plan
01

Chiropractic Treatment

Precise, hands-on spinal and joint adjustments to restore alignment, relieve pressure on the nervous system, and improve mobility.

Treatment is combined with mobility training and posture correction for results that last, not just temporary relief.

  • Hands-on assessment before any adjustment is made
  • Targeted joint mobilisation, not a generic routine
  • Rehabilitation and posture-correction exercises
  • Most patients feel looser within the first few sessions
02

DECOM Therapy

Decompression therapy gently relieves pressure on the spine and discs — a non-invasive option for chronic back and neck pain, often used alongside chiropractic adjustments.

  • Gentle traction, fully controlled and adjustable
  • Aimed at disc-related and nerve-related pain
  • Comfortable — most patients relax or doze off
03

Fascia Needling

Targeted needling releases tight fascia and muscle tension, helping speed up recovery and reduce stiffness — especially useful for patients dealing with chronic tightness or sports-related injuries.

  • Fine, sterile needles at specific tension points
  • A brief sensation, not a lingering pain
  • Often paired with stretching for faster release
04

Ultrasound Therapy

Deep-tissue ultrasound reduces inflammation and eases soft-tissue pain, supporting faster healing between visits.

  • A handheld probe delivers deep, gentle heat
  • Painless — most patients only feel light warmth
  • Speeds up recovery for soft-tissue injuries
