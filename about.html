Jason Wong, D.C. Chiropractor
Profile Clinical Experience Treatment Philosophy Patient Reviews
BSc (Hons) Chiropractic Certified Sports Injury Practitioner

Jason is a dedicated chiropractor focused on helping patients move better, live pain-free, and regain confidence in their bodies. He believes in evidence-based, non-invasive care that addresses both pain and movement quality.

CLINICAL EXPERIENCE

Hands-on experience in Johor Bahru

Jason gained valuable hands-on experience practicing in JB, where he treated a wide variety of patients — from office workers with chronic neck and lower back pain to active individuals and physically demanding workers with sports-related injuries. This exposure allowed him to refine a practical and adaptable treatment approach.

TREATMENT PHILOSOPHY

A practical, patient-centred approach

His experience highlighted how posture, work habits, and movement patterns play a major role in long-term pain. Jason focuses on identifying the root cause of discomfort rather than simply managing symptoms.

By combining chiropractic adjustments with mobility training, posture correction, and rehabilitation exercises, Jason helps patients achieve sustainable results. His goal is to support long-term health, improved function, and a better quality of life.

Max Lim, D.C. Chiropractor
Profile Clinical Experience Treatment Philosophy Patient Reviews
Chiropractic Specialist Practising Since 2015

Max is a chiropractor with a decade of clinical experience, blending evidence-based chiropractic care with insights drawn from traditional bone-setting to help patients move with more confidence.

CLINICAL EXPERIENCE

A decade of hands-on experience in Penang

Max has practised since 2015, caring for patients across Penang with neck, back, knee, and joint concerns. His own history of recurring knee injuries as a young athlete gave him a first-hand understanding of chronic musculoskeletal pain, and continues to shape how he works with patients facing similar challenges today.

TREATMENT PHILOSOPHY

Bridging Eastern and Western approaches

Max's own recovery was shaped in part by a family member's traditional bone-setting practice, an experience that left a lasting impression and continues to inform his clinical philosophy today. Rather than relying on a single method, he draws on both Eastern and Western principles to build a treatment plan suited to each patient's condition and goals.

His approach combines chiropractic adjustments with soft tissue therapy and other complementary modalities, aiming to restore movement and support long-term wellbeing rather than simply ease pain.
